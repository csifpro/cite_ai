01 / About me

A little about who I am

I'm a creative developer and designer based in Berlin, with a passion for building digital products that feel as good as they look.

With 6+ years of experience, I bridge the gap between design and engineering — translating complex ideas into intuitive, polished interfaces. I care deeply about the details: the micro-interactions, the typography, the performance, and the feeling a product leaves you with.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new design trends, experimenting with generative art, or capturing cityscapes with my camera.
